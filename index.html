Cockpit

PORTamidshipsSTBD
With sternway the helm reverses: tiller to port turns the bow to port.
Numpad 4 = tiller to port (bow turns to starboard) / Numpad 6 = tiller to starboard (bow turns to port). Dragging the bar above does the same.
Numpad 5 = rudder to amidships and take the helm back. Numpad 0 = let go of the tiller (freed, the rudder weathercocks with no force on the blade). Numpad Enter = lash/unlash.
Deck schematic (fixed scale, bow up). Every cleat prints its key. Drag a cleat toward its clew to ease, away to haul -- same rate-limited haul as the keys. Hover a cleat for its ease value.
